# Historical-defect validation

The recorded protocol was fixed on 2026-08-08. Public maintainer issue, pull-request, and revision records define symptoms and successful repaired behavior independently of the contract identities. The search frame includes the eligible model repositories and their PyTorch and Hugging Face Accelerate orchestration dependencies. Terms cover distributed evaluation/inference, validation duplication, last batches, gather metrics, missing samples, wrong metrics, rank seeds, main processes, samplers, truncation, and deadlocks.

Eligible records must describe incorrect distributed inference or evaluation behavior and provide a maintainer diagnosis, merged fix, or reproducible change that can be represented by a small CPU fixture. Training-only convergence issues, accelerator kernel issues, installation problems, feature requests, unverified reports, and duplicate patches are excluded. The four contract identities do not define the source labels.

For each record, the fixture first checks source-derived pre-fix and source-fixed predicates, then compares traces with the checker. The recorded targets were at least five independent defects from at least two ecosystems, all source assertions passing, detection of at least 70%, and no rejection of a source-fixed fixture.

All ten source assertions pass and all five pre-fix cases are detected. Four source-fixed cases are accepted; the sampling remedy discards a tail token and is rejected under the full-set contract. Thus the original zero-fixed-alert target is not met. The manuscript reports this result as a distinction between the maintainer's duplicate-removal objective and the declared evaluation population; it is not counted as universal successful repair acceptance.

Exact public records and fixture identifiers appear in `data/historical_defect_registry.csv`. Identifiers in this registry link reproducible test cases, not research workflow stages.
